Crickfunds

Privacy Policy and Account Deletion
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Crickfunds is an app that helps a sports team (such as a cricket team) keep track of a shared fund: match fees, player balances, fines, payments and party expenses. This page explains what information the app uses, who can see it, and how you can delete your account.

App publisher: [KM MEENASKHI]  ·  Contact: [meenaskhikashyap7514@gmail.com]

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy it is used
Google account name, email address and a unique sign-in IDTo sign you in and show your team who you are.
Mobile number (optional). A team admin may also type in a player's number.So the team admin can contact you, and to send WhatsApp reminders from the admin's own phone.

We do not collect your location, your contact list, or your payment card or bank details. Crickfunds does not process or hold money. When you pay, the app opens your own UPI app (such as Google Pay or PhonePe), and the payment happens there. Choosing a contact's number uses Android's contact picker, which gives the app only the one number you pick.

2. Who can see your information

  • Team members you belong to can see the team's records: player names, balances, matches, fines, payments, photos and phone numbers stored in the team's data.
  • Team admins can also see the name, email address and mobile number of people who join the team, and the change history.
  • A team admin can approve or remove members. Someone who only has the team code cannot see any team data until an admin approves them.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

3. Services we use

Crickfunds uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging and Cloud Functions) and Google Sign-In to store data, sign you in and send notifications. Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is limited by security rules, so only approved team members can read a team's data. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

Some data is also kept on your phone (a copy of your team's data so the app works offline, and your settings). Backup files that a team admin saves to the phone's Downloads folder are stored on that phone and are under the admin's control.

4. How long we keep information

Team records are kept for as long as the team uses the app, so that balances and history stay correct. A team admin can archive or delete old records. Your account information is removed when you delete your account (see below).

5. Delete your account and data

In the app: open Account settings and tap Delete my account. If you are the only team admin, first make another member a team admin.

By email: if you cannot use the app, write to [meenaskhikashyap7514@gmail.com] from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Crickfunds account". We will process the request within 30 days.

What is deleted:

  • Your Google sign-in link to Crickfunds (your login account)
  • Your membership of the team, including the name, email and mobile number stored with it
  • Your own profile photo
  • Your notification token

What stays with the team: the team's financial records belong to the team. Your player entry, balance, matches, fines, payment history, "I paid" messages and your name in the change history stay so that the team's books remain correct. On request, the team admin can remove your player entry, and we can delete the remaining records that identify you, except where they are needed to keep other members' balances correct.

6. Children

Crickfunds is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example when we add new features), we will update the date at the top of this page.
